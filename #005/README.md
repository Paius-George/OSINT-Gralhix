# Exercise #005

In this exercise we are given a screenshot from a live camera at a zoo. We are also told that the photo was taken on January 15, 2023, around 2 PM. We have to answer the following questions:

a) In which zoo are the polar bears located?

b) What was the temperature when the photo was taken?

c) What are the exact coordinates of where the polar bears were lying on the ground?
